## EXAMPLE (fictional — delete before use)

**Last updated:** 2026-09-14 by Елена

### Who is who

| Name | Relation | Language | Role | In the project? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Мария Ивановна | Patient | Russian | — | Yes |
| Елена | Daughter | Russian, Hebrew, English | Primary caregiver, maintains these files | Yes |
| Дмитрий | Grandson | Hebrew, English, some Russian | Helps with technology, lives nearby | Yes |
| Rina | Neighbour | Hebrew, Russian | Has a key, checks in | No |

### How the patient actually uses this

- Preferred channel: voice messages, usually in the morning after breakfast.
- Typical time: 08:00–10:00, and sometimes late evening when she cannot sleep.
- Easy: speaking, taking a photo of one object.
- Hard: typing, photographing a whole A4 page in focus, reading anything on screen without glasses.
- Annoys her: being asked the same thing twice, being asked for dates, long answers she has to scroll.

### Communication preferences

- Avoid: the word "деменция" unless a doctor has said it; anything that sounds like she is being checked up on.
- Reassures her: being told plainly what is normal for her specifically; hearing that something has been written down so nobody will forget it.
- Route to family: anything about prognosis, anything found in a document she has not seen, anything about her driving or living alone.

### Observations from visits

| Date | Observed by | What was noticed |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-08-30 | Елена | Fridge had very little fresh food. She says she is not hungry in the mornings. |
| 2026-09-06 | Дмитрий | She held the stair rail on both flights, did not do that in spring. |
| 2026-09-13 | Елена | Sharp and cheerful. Asked good questions about her lab results. |

### Practical context

- She cooks for herself; simple food, lots of soup, bread, tea. Green vegetables vary a lot week to week — relevant to warfarin.
- Pill organiser filled by Елена every Sunday evening.
- Дмитрий drives her to appointments.
- No paid carer at present.

### Questions the family already asked the doctor

| Question | Answer | When |
|---|---|---|
| Can she take something for knee pain? | Paracetamol only. No NSAIDs at all. | 2026-05-12, Dr. Levin |
| Is the dizziness dangerous? | Watch it; recheck potassium; stand up slowly. | 2026-09-15, Dr. Levin |

### Things the assistant should not ask the patient

- Exact dates of past events — she finds this distressing and the answers are unreliable. Ask Елена.
- Her full medication list — it is in the project; asking implies she should have memorised it.
- Anything requiring her to read small print on a package insert.
- Whether she has been eating properly — she will say yes. Ask about specific meals instead, gently, and rarely.
